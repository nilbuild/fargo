Streamif

A native macOS streaming studio. Go live on several platforms at once, record in 4K, and build your scene on the GPU.

Download for macOS View source

Go live everywhere at once

Every destination gets its own connection, resolution and bitrate, all from a single render pass. YouTube takes the full 4K feed while the rest take 1080p.

YouTube 4K Twitch Kick Facebook X Any RTMP server

Everything in one app

The things you do on every stream, without a second tool running.

Camera and screen

Any camera, any display or window, media files, images and GIFs.

Scenes on a canvas

Camera, screen, picture in picture and media, or place sources freely.

Record in 4K

HEVC straight to disk while you stream, at full quality.

Composited on the GPU

One 4K Metal texture at 60fps, so the CPU stays free for encoding.

Blur the background

On-device person segmentation, so no green screen is needed.

Overlays

Text, images, media and chat, placed anywhere on the canvas.

Live chat

YouTube and Twitch chat in a panel, or featured on the stream.

Live captions

On-device speech recognition, rendered as a styled overlay.

Mix your audio

Mic and system audio with a noise gate, EQ, compressor and soundboard.

Built for the Mac

No FFmpeg, no browser runtime, no server. RTMP, FLV muxing and AMF0 are written straight against VideoToolbox and Network.framework.
